## Description
ScopeAI is an autonomous competitive intelligence service for small businesses and agencies. A customer fills a brief, pays via Stripe, and three Gemini 2.5 Flash agents (Investigator → Analyst → Writer) research the live web with Google Search grounding and deliver a cited intelligence report in ~3 minutes — zero humans in the operational loop.

Built entirely on Google Cloud: Cloud Run, Vertex AI, Firestore, Cloud Build + Artifact Registry.
