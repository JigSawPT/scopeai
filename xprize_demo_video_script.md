# ScopeAI — XPRIZE 3-Minute Demonstration Video Script

**Target Video Duration:** 2 minutes 50 seconds (Under the 3-minute strict limit)  
**Required Focus:** Demonstrate live agent execution in production, autonomous decision making, and real user dashboard.

---

## Timeline & Shot Breakdown

### [0:00 - 0:25] Introduction & The Problem
* **Visual:** On-screen host/screencast showing a traditional $10,000 consulting invoice alongside a complex 80-page competitor PDF.
* **Audio:** "Small businesses lose to giant incumbents every single day because they can't afford $10,000 consulting firms or spend 40 hours manually analyzing competitors. Welcome to ScopeAI — the world’s first competitive intelligence agency operated 100% by autonomous AI agents on Google Cloud."

### [0:25 - 1:00] Ordering & Real-Time Checkout
* **Visual:** Screencast navigating to `https://scopeai.app`, filling out an order brief for a real business (e.g. "Acme Analytics vs. Mixpanel & Amplitude"), selecting the $99 Professional Tier, and completing checkout.
* **Audio:** "The customer submits their business goals in plain language. Once Stripe confirms payment via webhook, zero human hands touch the order. ScopeAI’s autonomous Gemini 3.6 Flash agent pipeline takes over immediately."

### [1:00 - 2:00] Live Agent Telemetry & Autonomous Execution
* **Visual:** Transition to `https://scopeai.app/report/[id]`. Zoom in on the live streaming Agent Execution Console showing real-time timestamps and status badges.
* **Highlight 1 (1:05 - 1:20):** "Watch the **Investigator Agent** query Gemini 2.5 Flash with Google Search grounding to extract competitor pricing models, feature gaps, and negative customer sentiment from public reviews."
* **Highlight 2 (1:20 - 1:40):** "Next, the **Analyst Agent** takes over, cross-referencing market positioning and synthesizing strategic SWOT matrices and unaddressed market opportunities."
* **Highlight 3 (1:40 - 2:00):** "Finally, the **Writer Agent** drafts an executive-level Markdown briefing report and emails it directly to the client."

### [2:00 - 2:30] Deliverable Quality & Financial Proof
* **Visual:** Screencast scrolling through the finished deliverable report on screen (Executive Summary card, SWOT breakdown, actionable recommendations). Show Stripe Dashboard export and Google Cloud Run console with active billing metrics.
* **Audio:** "Here is the final deliverable. High-grade strategic intelligence produced in under 30 seconds at over 90% gross margin, running on Google Cloud Run with complete telemetry observability."

### [2:30 - 2:50] Conclusion & XPRIZE Vision
* **Visual:** ScopeAI logo with "Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash on Vertex AI | Built for Build with Gemini XPRIZE".
* **Audio:** "ScopeAI proves that small teams powered by Gemini agents can deliver operations that used to take entire departments. Thank you."
